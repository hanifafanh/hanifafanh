## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. 🪖 Helmet Detection on Motorcyclists: YOLOv12 vs RT-DETR
A deep learning project that compares two modern object detectors, **YOLOv12** and **RT-DETR**, on recognizing helmet use among motorcycle riders to support traffic safety monitoring.

- **Models:** YOLOv12 and RT-DETR as the main models, benchmarked against Faster R-CNN, YOLOv8, and YOLOv11.
- **Workflow:** Followed the **CRISP-DM** framework, starting with dataset exploration (class balance, image quality, duplicate checks), then preprocessing and model training.
- **Dataset:** Self-collected helmet and no-helmet images, including tricky cases such as riders in hoodies, beanies, or bicycle helmets.
- **Tech:** Python, PyTorch, YOLOv12, RT-DETR

### 2. 🧠 MentalUX: Online Psychology Consultation Platform
A website that connects users with verified psychologists. Built by a team of 5 for the **E-Business** and **User Experience** courses, covering business analysis, UX research, system design, and user validation.

**The problem:** Indonesia has about **1 clinical psychologist per 91,900 people** (the WHO benchmark is 1 per 30,000), and most are concentrated on Java. MentalUX aims to widen access to care and supports **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being**.

- **Features:** Psychologist listings with transparent pricing, consultation scheduling and categories, QRIS/bank transfer payments, real-time chat, educational articles and videos, a satisfaction survey, and **MentalUX AI**, a chatbot for early support.
- **Role-based access:** Separate flows for **Customers**, **Psychologists** (certificate upload, schedule, transactions), and **Admins** (psychologist verification, transactions, article management), realized in a Figma prototype of 27+ screens.
- **UX research:** Applied **Design Thinking** (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) with a 5/5/5 approach (5 journals, 5 similar websites, 5 interviewees), plus surveys, observation, personas, and wireframes revised from user feedback.
- **Business & innovation:** **Business Model Canvas**, competitor analysis, customer segmentation, SWOT, value proposition, revenue and cost projections, and innovation and marketing strategy.
- **System design:** Waterfall SDLC with use case and activity diagrams, plus a security plan.
- **Tech:** Figma, Design Thinking, BMC

### 3. 🏍️ CRT APP: Online Sales Mobile App for an Adventure Motorcycle Dealer
A mobile app design for **CRT (Cilangkap Racing Team)**, a dealer of KTM, Husqvarna, and Sherco motocross and adventure bikes. Built by a team of 4 for the collaboration between **IS Analysis and Design** and **Database Systems**.

**The problem:** CRT sold only offline, which limited its reach to nearby customers. Manual record-keeping also caused stock and price errors, and the sales process was not integrated.

- **Features:** Catalog (motorcycles, spare parts, accessories), category search, wishlist, cart, checkout, QRIS/bank transfer payments, product reviews and ratings, and stock management for admins.
- **Analysis & design:** Use Case, Activity, and Class Diagrams, along with functional and non-functional requirements.
- **UI/UX:** An interactive **Figma** prototype of the full purchase flow, from sign-up to order confirmation.
- **Methodology:** Prototyping, with user feedback driving each revision.
- **User testing results:** 94.3% said the app meets mobile e-commerce standards, 97.1% found it relevant, 88.6% liked the color scheme, and the average rating was **4.47 / 5**. Revisions added **Dark Mode** and a cart quantity adjuster.

### 4. 🌍 Global Commodity Trade Analysis & Visualization (2010–2016) — *BI & Dashboard Developer*
An analysis of world trade data from the **UN Comtrade** dataset, visualized in Tableau.

- **Data preparation:** Filtered the data to 2010–2016, cleaned missing and duplicate records, and created a `Trade Direction` calculated field that maps imports and exports into inbound and outbound flows.
- **Three core dashboards:** (1) trade distribution across countries through a choropleth map and ranking bar chart, (2) trade value trends over time through a multi-series line chart, area chart, and highlight table, and (3) dominant commodity composition through a bubble chart and category distribution.
- **Data storytelling:** Dynamic filters, tooltips, and visual highlights combined into a single Tableau Story that explores trade concentration, food security commodities, and market fluctuations across countries.

### 5. 🎓 Data Warehouse for Scopus Publication Analytics
A data warehouse and Power BI project that merges lecturer master data with Scopus export files at UMN (2020–2025) into a single source of truth for tracking research performance. Built as a team for the **IS545** course.

- **ETL & data quality:** An end-to-end ETL pipeline in **Power Query**, covering data cleansing (name standardization, removing duplicate lecturers and publications) and harmonizing publication types, venues, and CiteScore quartiles.
- **Dimensional modeling:** A **star schema** with fact tables (FactPublication, FactPublicationAuthor) and dimensions (lecturer, study program, faculty, venue, publication type, date), complete with surrogate keys and Slowly Changing Dimensions.
- **Dashboards:** A **Power BI** semantic model and interactive reports on quartile performance by faculty, publication trends by study program, and collaboration across programs.

### 6. 🩺 Obesity Level Classification: KNN + Gemini AI
A **Streamlit** web app that estimates a person's obesity level from physical data and daily habits. A **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)** model makes the prediction, and **Google Gemini** then turns the result into a health risk overview and lifestyle suggestions in Indonesian. Built together with a team.

- **Model:** A scikit-learn pipeline that combines preprocessing (StandardScaler for numeric features, OneHotEncoder for categorical ones) with KNN (k = 3, Manhattan distance, distance-based weighting). It classifies **7 weight categories**, from insufficient weight to Obesity Type III.
- **Data:** The *Estimation of Obesity Levels Based on Eating Habits and Physical Condition* dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository, with 16 attributes such as height, weight, eating habits, physical activity, and mode of transportation.
- **AI integration:** Gemini 2.5 Flash receives the prediction along with BMI and the user's habits, then produces two key health risks and three practical recommendations.
- **Interface:** An interactive form, per-class probabilities, and automatic BMI calculation as extra context.
- **Note:** Built for educational purposes, not as a medical diagnosis tool.
- **Tech:** Python, Streamlit, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, Google Gemini
